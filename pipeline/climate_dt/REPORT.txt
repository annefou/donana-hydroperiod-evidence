CLIMATE DT FITNESS TEST - DONANA POND HYDROPERIOD DRIVERS
Site: Donana, Natura 2000 ES0000024 (SW Spain, ~36.8-37.2 N, 6.6-6.1 W). Run 2026-10-22.
Prototype test: one site, one realization per model, 34 hydrological years. Not an operational product.

VERDICT
  Usable after bias correction, not as-is. IFS-NEMO is close as-is for annual totals but has the wrong
  timing within the year. IFS-FESOM is not usable raw: it is too dry (-23 %) and has 20/34 "dry" years
  against about 7 expected. Fit the correction separately for each model.
  After monthly scaling of rainfall and of daily Tmean/Tmax/Tmin (fitted HY 1990-2013), both models
  reproduce the observed distribution of hydrological-year rainfall, the Oct-Mar share, the dry- and
  wet-year frequencies and the evaporative demand. They do this within the gap between E-OBS and ERA5,
  and they hold out of sample (HY 2014-2023).
  Use both models (and E-OBS/ERA5 as reference) as a 2-member scenario envelope, not one model.

WHAT WAS COMPARED
  Hydrological year (HY) y = 1 Sep y .. 31 Aug y+1; HY 1990..2023 (n = 34).
  Climate DT generation 2, realization 1. hist (activity "baseline") 1990-2014 + SSP3-7.0 ("projections")
  2015-2024. IFS-NEMO (MareNostrum5) and IFS-FESOM (LUMI). Hourly avg_tprate, 2t and avg_ie from the
  Polytope timeseries feature at 5 spherical HEALPix level-10 cell centres (nside 1024, NESTED):
    3490355 marsh centre (36.935 N, 6.328 W)   3490408 marsh north (37.122, -6.240)
    3490357 marsh east  (36.982, -6.196)       3490451 aquifer / El Rocio (37.122, -6.504)
    3490362 Biological Reserve dunes (36.982, -6.460)
  Rainfall uses the mean of the 5 cells; temperature uses cells 3490355/3490451/3490362. The data were not regridded.
  Hourly values were aggregated to daily: rainfall as the daily mean rate x 86400; Tmean/Tmax/Tmin from the 24 hourly 2 m values.
  Observations:
    E-OBS v31.0e at 0.1 deg: the nearest land cell to each DT centre, 5 cells averaged. Primary reference for rainfall.
    ERA5 at 0.25 deg: the 5 points fall on 2 grid points (37.0 N, 6.25 W and 6.5 W). Primary reference for temperature, Tmax and PET.
  Evaporative demand: Hargreaves-Samani reference ET (FAO-56 eq. 52) from daily Tmean, Tmax and Tmin. It is
  computed the same way for DT and ERA5. The DT has no PET variable on Polytope: avg_pevr, mx2t and mn2t
  were refused ("datacube does not contain a date axis").
  Because DT years are not in phase with real years, the comparison uses statistics only.

HOW TO READ THIS FOR HYDROPERIOD
  - Annual rainfall distribution and dry/wet-year frequency drive whether ponds fill. After BC these match
    the observations: KS p = 0.98, 7-9 dry years against 7 observed.
  - Rainfall timing (Oct-Mar share 0.79-0.80 against 0.75-0.78 observed) is acceptable. Raw NEMO shifts
    rain from Sep/Oct into Feb-Apr. Monthly scaling corrects this on average.
  - Drought persistence cannot be tested with n = 34. Lag-1 autocorrelation is about 0 in all series, and
    the longest dry run is 1-2 years. Raw FESOM runs of 4 dry years come from its dry bias.
  - Evaporative demand: raw PET is 10-12 % too high. Corrected PET is within 0.5 % of ERA5. Tmax must be
    corrected on its own; correcting only Tmean leaves PET +13 % too high. The annual maximum Tmax stays
    +2.2 to +2.5 K above ERA5 after BC. ERA5 itself underestimates point heat extremes, so this is not
    resolved here.
  - The raw DT also gives its own actual evaporation (avg_ie, NEMO 432 mm/HY at the marsh-centre cell). It
    has no observational counterpart here and was not used.

LIMITS
  - One realization per model, and 34 unphased years. The observed 20th percentile has a bootstrap s.e.
    of 32 mm (95 % range 282-400 mm). Tests of CV and autocorrelation have little power.
  - The observations disagree with each other: ERA5 is 9 % wetter than E-OBS, 0.5 K warmer, and has 62
    against 55 wet days. E-OBS station density in the Donana marsh is low. The AEMET ROCIO_IBEB 5 km grid
    and the Palacio de Donana station (EBD-CSIC ICTS-RBD) would be better references but were not used
    (no keyless download).
  - Only 2 of 3 models were tested: ICON was not requested.
  - The validation decade is 4 months of hist plus SSP3-7.0. The forcings differ little before 2025.
  - Monthly scaling corrects means only. Daily-intensity and sub-monthly timing errors (they matter for runoff
    to ponds) remain. BC2 quantile mapping fixes the daily distribution per month but not the persistence.
  - The point extraction uses spherical HEALPix centres. The geodetic offset is up to 0.13 deg, so a cell can
    sit about 14 km from the WGS84 location. This does not matter at the 5-30 km scales compared here.
  - Hargreaves PET is a temperature-only proxy. It ignores radiation, wind and humidity changes.

EURO-CORDEX VIA AdapteCCa (documentation only: escenarios.adaptecca.es/doc/datos.pdf, v7.0, 13 June 2025)
  - 46 EURO-CORDEX GCM-RCM runs (codes E2-E47) plus the ensemble mean. All 46 have RCP8.5 and 19 have RCP4.5
    (CMIP5 forcing, no SSPs). EUR-11 is about 12 km.
  - Delivered bias-adjusted with ISIMIP3 (Lange 2021) on the AEMET ROCIO_IBEB 5 km observational grid. The
    reference period is 1971-2000.
  - The viewer serves annual, seasonal and monthly values and indices, including potential evapotranspiration.
    Raw (unadjusted) CORDEX is only in a legacy viewer version. CMIP6 statistical downscaling (DeepESD etc.)
    is also offered on the 5 km grid.
  - Comparison: AdapteCCa gives a 46-member, already bias-adjusted, 5 km ensemble on Spain's own
    observational grid. The DT gives 2 testable members (3 models) at about 5 km native resolution, with
    hourly output and SSP3-7.0 only. For scenario envelopes and dry-year probabilities, EURO-CORDEX/AdapteCCa
    is the stronger base. The DT is useful as a high-resolution, hourly, physically consistent complement
    (storm timing, evaporation fluxes). It needs the bias correction above.

REPRODUCE (from this folder; ROOT = this folder)
  1. python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
  2. Credentials: ~/.polytopeapirc (DestinE, upgraded access for generation 2) and ~/.cdsapirc (CDS; accept
     the ERA5 licence once on the CDS website). E-OBS needs no account.
  3. sh scripts/01_fetch_dt_all.sh "$PWD"      (72 Polytope requests, about 1 h with 6 in parallel; resumable)
  4. .venv/bin/python -I scripts/02_fetch_era5.py "$PWD"
  5. .venv/bin/python -I scripts/03_fetch_eobs.py "$PWD"   (HTTP range reads, about 1 min)
  6. .venv/bin/python -I scripts/04_analyse.py "$PWD"      -> results.json, data/derived/
  7. .venv/bin/python -I scripts/05_figures.py "$PWD"      -> figures/
